Uni prototypes

You and I: an extended mind you can install. These are playable sketches of where Uni could go. Tap around, break things, then tell Uni what felt right and what didn't.

01Uni vault

One vault, six folders. Watch a capture arrive, get linked, and come back later when you ask by meaning rather than by keyword.

Try: tap “+ Capture”, then ask the vault “what was I worried about with the garden?”

Feedback wanted: do these six folders make sense to a stranger? Does the answer-with-sources feel trustworthy?

02Uni setup

The packaging story. A new person installs Uni: picks self-hosted or hosted, connects a vault, chooses channels, makes a first capture, and meets their agent.

Try: go through all six steps and import a “Tana export” on the way.

Feedback wanted: which step would lose people? Is the privacy step clear enough to trust?

04Parachute, next: Uni's memory layer

One vault as a living map. Captures sit at a sacred core that is never rewritten or shared. Around them: people, projects, meetings, life threads. Search by meaning and see why each result matched.

Try: scrub the timeline, ask “when did I feel stretched too thin?”, save a capture and accept Uni's proposed links, then share only #co-op with Ines and watch the captures lock.

Feedback wanted: are the “why this matched” reasons trustworthy? Does the capture-vs-derived boundary feel safe enough to hand an agent the rest?
