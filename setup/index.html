Welcome

Uni is a second mind that's yours.

u·n·i means you and I. You talk, write and record. Uni remembers, connects the dots, and holds you to what you said you'd do. It's three open parts working together:

🪂
A vaultYour memory. Plain notes you own and can export any time.
✦
An agentReads the vault, does the work, writes back what it learns.
💬
ChannelsWhere you talk to it: the app, chat, email, voice.
Step 1 · Where it lives

Where should your Uni run?

Same software either way. You can move later: the vault goes with you.

Step 2 · Your vault

Make a home for your memory.

Six folders. Simple enough that you'll always know where something is.

📁 Captures/your raw words, never rewritten
📁 Meetings/transcripts from calls
📁 People/who matters and why
📁 Projects/what you're working on
📁 Uni/how your agent runs, editable
📁 Archive/nothing is ever deleted
⬆ Tana / Roam export
⬆ Obsidian or Markdown folder
⬆ Voice memos (we'll transcribe them)
🔒 Meaning search runs on a small model inside your vault. Your notes are never sent anywhere to be indexed.

Pick as many as you like. You can add more later.
